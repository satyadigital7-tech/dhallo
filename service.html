Eligibility Criteria

Loan Documentation & Requirements Overview

We assist salaried employees, self-employed professionals, and registered businesses with minimal documentation.

Salaried Individuals

  • Stable Monthly Income
  • Employment ID & Salary Slips
  • KYC (Aadhaar & PAN Card)
  • 6 Months Bank Statement

Self-Employed Professionals

  • Business Proof / License
  • 2-3 Years ITR with Computation
  • Current & Savings Bank Statement
  • KYC Documents

Registered Businesses

  • GST Registration & Filing
  • Audited Financial Statements
  • Company PAN & MOA/AOA
  • Bank Statements (12 Months)
